JARVIS is an Iron Man–style console you can talk to, built around one rule: every figure on screen is measured, not simulated. Real CPU cores, real GPU temperatures, the real devices on your network, the real weather where you are.

You speak or type, and answers stream in and are spoken as they're written. Research lives as threads on a board you can group, link and tidy by voice. Web search brings back sources, pictures and video inside the thread.

It runs on your own key: Gemini, which has a free tier, or ChatGPT. Connect both and it falls back from one to the other, and says why.
